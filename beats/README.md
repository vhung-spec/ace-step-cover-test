# Beats for Vietnamese songs

Instrumental beats (no vocal) for Vietnamese songs, played on free sampled
instruments by the same engine as `../bossa/` (MIDI per instrument, sfizz,
`../render_samples.py`), rendered by `.github/workflows/bossa.yml` (the styles
in `render.txt`) to the `bossa-renders` branch.

- `bolero.py`: bolero (nhạc vàng): the guitar's rải or quạt patterns over
  the bass on 1 and 3, maracas and bongos (or a soft kit for nhạc sống), and
  the guitar phím lõm's intro and answers (pitch bends for nhấn and rung) on
  Karoryfer's Emilyguitar, or a tenor saxophone. The sketches play an
  original tune, Phố Nhỏ Mưa Thưa (A minor, 66 bpm); bolero-khong-loi is the
  instrumental version with the guitar singing it. bolero-trong-that and
  bolero-trong-that-rai play it on a real kit (Versilian's Virtuosity
  Drums: the cross-stick's bolero groove, fills into each phrase's end and
  each section, a crash on its first beat) with congas and a shaker from
  the same room; bolero-trong-loop is the drums and percussion alone.

Instruments: the University of Iowa's nylon guitar, Karoryfer's Meatbass,
Fashionbass, Emilyguitar and Big Rusty Drums (CC0), VCSL's maracas, bongos
and tenor saxophone, VSCO-2-CE's strings, Versilian's Virtuosity Drums
(CC0).
